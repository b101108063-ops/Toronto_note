Aortic Dissection

Definition

tear in aortic intima allowing blood to dissect into the media

Stanford classification: Type A (involving the ascending aorta) vs Type B (involving the aorta distal to the
left subclavian artery)

acute <2 wk (initial mortality 1% per h for Type A dissections)

chronic >2 wk

Etiology
* most common: chronic and/or uncontrolled HTN

+ other: connective tissue disease (e.g. Marfan syndrome, Ehlers-Danlos type IV syndrome), cystic medial
necrosis, atherosclerosis, congenital conditions (e.g. coarctation of aorta, bicuspid aortic valves, patent
ductus arteriosus), infection (e.g. syphilis), trauma, arteritis (e.g. Takayasu's)
