Investigations
+ lymphoscintigraphy: most definitive test

Treatment

* conservative measures: avoid limb injury, treat skin infections early, skin hygiene, limb elevation, avoid
prolonged sitting/standing/crossing legs

+ external support: intensive (compression bandages) vs. maintenance (compression garments)

+ exercise: gentle daily exercise of affected limb, gradually increasing range of motion (must wear
compression garment while exercising)

* massage: manual lymph drainage therapy

+ surgical: physiological (early disease: increase lymphatic drainage) lymphovenous bypass vs. reductive
(advanced disease: remove fibroadipose deposits)
