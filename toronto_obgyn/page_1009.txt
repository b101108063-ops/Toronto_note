Screening

+ 10-item Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale is the most widely used screening tool

+ other options: self-administered nine-item Patient Health Questionnaire, 20-item Center for Epidemiologic
Studies Depression Scale

Treatment

+ structured psychotherapy as first-line treatment for mild to moderate cases with no prior history of major
depression

+ antidepressants, psychotherapy, supportive care, and electroconvulsive therapy if refractory or if features of
psychosis, catatonia, high risk of suicide, and fluid or food refusal leading to dehydration and malnutrition

Prognosis

+ may be associated with altered fetal physiologic effects, adverse pregnancy and neonatal outcomes,
abnormal infant and child development, or cognitive impairment and psychopathology in the offspring,
leading to lasting long-term effects

+ increased risk of recurrence after pregnancy, and conversion of the diagnosis to bipolar disorder

Multi-Fetal Gestation and Fetal Malpresentation

Definitions
+ multi-fetal gestation: two or more embryos/fetuses occupying the uterus simultaneously
+ fetal malpresentation: when fetus is not head-down as birth approaches
