Twin-Twin Transfusion Syndrome

Definition
+ formation of placental intertwin vascular anastomoses that can cause arterial blood from donor twin to
pass into veins of the recipient twin

Epidemiology
+ 10% of monochorionic twins
+ concern if >30% discordance in EFW

Clinical Features

+ donor twin: IUGR, hypovolemia, hypotension, anemia, and oligohydramnios

+ recipient twin: hypervolemia, HTN, CHF, polycythemia, edema, polyhydramnios, and kernicterus in
neonatal period

Investigations
+ detected by U/S screening, Doppler flow analysis

Management

+ fetoscopic laser ablation of placental vascular anastomoses (preferred between 16-26 wk GA)

+ therapeutic serial amniocentesis to decompress polyhydramnios of recipient twin and decrease pressure in
cavity and on placenta

+ intrauterine blood transfusion to donor twin if necessary
