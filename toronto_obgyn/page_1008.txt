Antenatal Depression

Definition

+ major depression occurring in a patient who is pregnant, onset may be prior to pregnancy

Epidemiology
+ occurs in 7-9% of pregnancies

Risk Factors

+ prior history of depression, anxiety, unintended or unwanted pregnancy, life stress, intimate partner
violence or history of abuse, poor social support, chronic general medical conditions (specifically

hypothyroidism)

Clinical Features

+ comparable to symptoms of non-pregnant major depressive disorder (see Psychiatry, PS10)
+ suspect if: prior history of depression, excessive anxiety about the fetus, poor self-esteem, despondency,

anhedonia, non-adherence to antenatal care, poor weight gain due to decreased appetite or inadequate diet,

suicidal ideation
