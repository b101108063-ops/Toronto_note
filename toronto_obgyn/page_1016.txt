+ increases risk of endometritis

maternal intrapartum GBS colonization during current pregnancy

+ risk of vertical transmission (neonatal sepsis, meningitis or pneumonia, and death)

Investigations

+ offer screening to all patients at 35-37 wk GA with vaginal and anorectal swabs for GBS culture

Treatment

+ prophylactic treatment of maternal GBS at delivery decreases neonatal morbidity and mortality

+ antibiotics for GBS prophylaxis

= penicillin G5 million IU IV, then 2.5 million IU IV q4 h until delivery
+ should be given 4h prior to delivery to be considered adequate
= penicillin allergy but low risk for anaphylaxis: cefazolin 2 g IV, then 1 g q8h

= vancomycin and clindamycin levels in amniotic fluid do not reach therapeutic levels, all babies should

be screened for GBS despite treatment
