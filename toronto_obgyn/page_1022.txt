Baseline FHR
= normal range is 110-160 bpm
= parameter of fetal well-being vs. distress
Variability
= physiologic variability is a normal characteristic of FHR
= variability is measured over a 15 min period and is described as: absent, minimal (<6 bpm), moderate
(6-25 bpm), or marked (>25 bpm)
= normal variability indicates fetal acid-base status is acceptable
= canonly be assessed by CTG
= variability decreases intermittently even in a healthy fetus
