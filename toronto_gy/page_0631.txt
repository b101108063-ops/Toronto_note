Treatment Guidelines
+ primary indication is treatment of menopausal symptoms (vasomotor instability)
= should not be prescribed if the only objective is the prevention of chronic disease
hormone therapy is safest when initiated <10 years after menopause and/or <60 years old
before starting, review the benefits and risks (see Table 19) and contraindications with the patient
use the lowest effective dose; patients on standard dose should be advised to lower dose after a few years
patients receiving MHT must be evaluated annually
decisions around duration of treatment should be individualized and not discontinued solely based upon
patient age; an extended use of MHT (beyond age 60 or >5 years of MHT use) may be reasonable when the
clinician and patient agree that the benefits of symptom relief outweigh the risks
= tapering and abruptly discontinuing MHT have similar impacts on symptom recurrence, but for
patients with a history of severe baseline vasomotor symptom, gradual tapering is probably preferable
