HIDRADENITIS SUPPURATIVA

Definition
+ achronic scarring inflammatory skin condition that is a result of follicular occlusion and inflammation
within intertriginous zones and areas rich in apocrine glands

‘al Features

+ primary lesions are inflammatory nodules

+ presence of sinus formation, clusters of open comedones (double tombstone comedones), and hypertrophic
scarring of intertriginous areas

sites: occurs primarily in the intertriginous areas of the axillae (most common site), inguinal area, inner
thighs, perianal and perineal areas, mammary and inframammary regions, buttocks, pubic region,
scrotum, vulva, trunk, and occasionally the scalp and retroauricular areas

additionally significant hyper pigmentation and keloid formation can be seen in more pigmented skin types

Pathophysiology

+ follicular occlusion, follicular rupture, and an immune response

+ HS may occur as part of the follicular occlusion tetrad (along with cicatricial alopecia, acne conglobata, and
pilonidal disease)

Epidemiology

+ affects 1-4% of the population, F>M

+ onset of symptoms occur between puberty and age 40, typically in 2nd or 3rd decade
increased incidence in people of African descent

associated with smoking and excess weight

Differential Diagnosis
+ folliculitis, furuncles, carbuncles, acne vulgaris, Crohn’s disease, granuloma inguinale, pyoderma
gangrenosum, recurrent pilonidal sinus, abscess

Investigations
+ dlinical diagnosis
+ ultrasound can be helpful for disease staging or detecting subclinical disease

Management

+ behavioural: avoidance of triggers including shaving or wearing tight-fitting clothing, smoking cessation,
weight management, and laser hair removal

pain management with NSAIDs

mild disease: local therapy with topical antibiotics, topical resorcinol, and intralesional steroids
moderate to severe disease: antibiotic therapy (oral tetracyclines, clindamycin and rifampin combination,
dapsone), oral retinoids, hormonal therapy (metformin, OCPs), surgery (deroofing), laser and light-based
therapies (CO: laser and Nd:YAG), and biologic therapy
