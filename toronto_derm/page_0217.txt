Clinical Features

surrounded by erythema

can present with bullae

+ common sites: face, arms, legs, and buttocks

Etiology
+ GAS, S. aureus, or both

Epidemiology
+ preschool and young adults living in crowded conditions, poor hygiene, neglected minor trauma
