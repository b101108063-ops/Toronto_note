Epidemiology

+ 25% of the population exercises regularly, 50% occasionally, 25% is sedentary

Management

+ ask about current activity level, assess readiness to make a change, advise about current guidelines and
benefits of exercise, agree on a goal, assist and arrange follow-up to re-assess
* encourage warm up and cool down periods to allow transition between rest and activity and to avoid

injuries

+ exercise with caution for patients with CAD, DM (risk of hypoglycemia), exercise-induced asthma
* patients with known CAD should have cardiac assessment prior to commencing exercise

+ benefits of exercise

= reduces risk of premature death, heart disease, stroke, HTN, certain types of cancer, T2DM,

osteoporosis, and overweight/obesity

= leads to improved fitness, strength, mobility, functional independence, and mental health (morale and

self-esteem)
