Figure 25. Approach to investigating gynecomastia

Treatment
+ initial observation for most men with gynecomastia (after stopping offending medications and treating
underlying cause)
+ medical
= correct the underlying disorder, discontinue responsible drug
= androgens for hypogonadism
= anti-estrogens: tamoxifen has most evidence for benefit
+ surgical
= longstanding (>12 mo, fibrotic), discomfort, or causing psychological distress
