Paraneoplastic Syndrome

Endocrinology

+ clinical syndromes involving non-metastatic systemic effects that accompany malignant disease

« triggered by antibodies against neoplasm cross-reacting with normal tissue or by production of a
physiologically active substance by the neoplasm

+ commonly present with cancers of lung, breast, ovaries, or lymphatic system

Investigations

+ CBC, electrolytes, creatinine, LFTs, ALP, erythrocyte sedimentation rate (ESR), CRP, serum/urine
electrophoresis

+ serum autoantibodies, lumbar puncture

+ imaging: skeletal survey, CT, MRI, positron emission tomography (PET) scan

+ + endoscopy

Treatment

+ treat underlying tumour: surgery, radiation, chemotherapy
+ treat immune-mediated disorder: intravenous immunoglobulin (IVIG), steroids, immunosuppressive
drugs, plasmapheresis (reserved for patients with identifiable antibodies in serum)
