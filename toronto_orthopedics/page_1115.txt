SPINAL STENOSIS

+ narrowing of spinal canal

+ congenital (idiopathic, osteopetrosis, achondroplasia) or acquired (degenerative, iatrogenic ~ post spinal
surgery, ankylosing spondylosis, Paget's disease, trauma)

Clinical Features

+ bilateral back and leg pain
+ neurogenic claudication

+ 4motor weakness

Investigations
+ CT/MRI reveals narrowing of spinal canal

Treatment
+ non-operative

= physiotherapy (flexion exercises, stretch/strength exercises), NSAIDs, lumbar epidural steroids
+ operative

= indication: non-operative failure >6 mo

= decompressive surgery
