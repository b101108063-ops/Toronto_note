Prognosis in COPD
+ prognostic factors
= frequency and severity of acute exacerbations is the single best predictor
= lung function tests and mMRC dyspnea scale add value
= development of hypoxemia, hypercapnia, or cor pulmonale portend a poor prognosis

= greater score = higher probability the patient will die from COPD; score can also be used to predict
hospitalization
= 10 point index consisting of four factors:
+ Body mass index (BMI): <21 (+1 point)
+ Obstruction (FEV:): 50-64% (+1), 36-49% (+2), <35% (+3)
