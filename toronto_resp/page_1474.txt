Definition
+ mechanical obstruction of the pulmonary vasculature leading to obstruction of blood flow
+ can be classified as acute, subacute, or chronic depending on presentation of symptoms relative to time
after initial event
= an acute PE can also be classified as massive, sub-massive, or low-risk PE

Etiology and Pathophysiology

+ lower extremity deep vein thrombi are the source of most clinically recognized PEs

+ less common causes include fat emboli, septic emboli, amniotic fluid emboli, tumour thrombi, and air
emboli

+ thrombi often start in calf, but must propagate into proximal veins to create a sufficiently large thrombus
for aclinically significant PE

Epidemiology

+ one of the most common causes of preventable death in the hospital

+ VTE affects approximately 1-2 in 1000 adults per yr with approximately one third of first VTE presentations
being due to PE
