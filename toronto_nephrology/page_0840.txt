Polyuria

Definition
+ output >3 L/d in adults and 2 L/m2 in children. Distinguish from urinary frequency, where urination
occurs multiple times per day but the total volume over 24 h is <3 L

Etiology
«+ either due to water diuresis or solute diuresis
+ solute diuresis:
= glucose (uncontrolled diabetes)
= salt (increased salt intake or IV fluids with sodium)
= urea (resolution of azotemia, varied intake of protein (e.g. excessive enteral or parenteral nutrition),
tissue breakdown)
+ water diuresis:
= diabetes insipidus (central or nephrogenic)
= polydipsia (excess water drinking)
= drugs (most commonly diuretics)

Clinical Features

+ must distinguish between true polyuria and urinary frequency

+ look for sources of external fluid intake (IV fluids, tube feedings)

+ assess for neurological changes (stroke, trauma, postoperative) (for central diabetes insipidus). Assess for
pituitary dysfunction
