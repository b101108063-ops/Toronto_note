+ cardiovascular system: cardiomyopathy, CHF, arrhythmia, pericarditis, atherosclerosis
+ hematologic: anemia, bleeding tendency (platelet dysfunction), infections
+ endocrine

«= hypertriglyceridemia, accelerated atherogenesis
+ metabolic: anion gap and non anion gap metabolic acidosis
+ renal osteodystrophy: secondary increased PTH due to decreased Ca**, high PO.*, and low active vitamin
D (1,25-OH vitamin D3)
«= osteitis fibrosa cystica
«+ dermatologic: pruritus, ecchymosis, hematoma, calciphylaxis (vascular Ca** deposition)

Treatment

« therapies include, dialysis, renal transplant or non-dialytic conservative management

+ renal transplant should be offered to all patients, if suitable; if the patient is not able to havea transplant or
prefers dialysis, then dialysis is preferred therapy

+ for patients who are unfit or choose not to do either transplant or dialysis, conservative non-dialytic
management is preferred

+ initiation of chronic dialysis has major implications on patients and healthcare system

+ there are 2 types of dialysis:
= blood based therapy - hemodialysis and hemodiaflitration (not done commonly)
= peritoneal dialysis (using body’s own peritoneal as filter membrane)
+ dialysis can be done in-centre (hospital) or at home (PD and home hemodialysis)
+ decision to start dialysis in ESRD should be symptom driven, and informed by electrolyte imbalances.
Dialysis generally started when GFR reaches approximately <10 mL/min
+ hemodialysis:
= blood is filtered across a semipermeable membrane removing accumulated toxic waste products,
solutes, excess fluid (ultrafiltration), and restoring buffering agents to the bloodstream
= available as in-centre treatments (intermittent (e.g. 3-6x/wk); can also be conducted nocturnally if at
home, the patient has 3-7 nights of dialysis; each session can be between 4-8 h
= vascular access can be achieved through a central venous catheter, an artificial arterio-venous (AV)
graft, or an AV fistula
« AV fistula consists of surgically connecting artery to vein, which increases vein diameter, and wall
thickness, allowing for easy cannulation to gain access to blood for hemodialysis
= patients with CKD should be referred for vascular access surgery to attempt construction of a primary
AV fistula when their eGFR is <15-20 mL/min, or within 1 yr of an anticipated need
+ check Kidney Failure Risk Equation, which provides the 2 and 5 year probability of treated kidney failure
for a potential patient with CKD stage 3 to 5
+ peritoneal dialysis: peritoneum acts as a semipermeable membrane similar to hemodialysis filter
= advantages: independence, fewer stringent dietary restrictions, better rehabilitation rates, allows the
patient to have therapy at home
= available as continuous ambulatory (CAPD; 2-5 exchanges/d) or automated PD (using cycler machine at
night)
+ refer patients with chronic renal disease to a nephrologist early on to facilitate treatment and plan in
advance for renal replacement therapy (RRT)
