SECONDARY CAUSES OF GLOMERULAR DISEASE

Amyloidosis
+ nodular deposits of amyloid in mesangium, usually related to amyloid light chain
+ presents as nephrotic range proteinuria (albuminuria) with progressive renal insufficiency

+ can be primary or secondary to multiple myeloma, TB, inflammatory diseases (e.g. rheumatoid arthritis),

or malignancy
