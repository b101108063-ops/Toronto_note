Sensory Exam

+ primary sensory modalities

. spinothalamic tract: pain and temperature

« dorsal column-medial lemniscus pathway: fine touch, vibration, proprioception
+ cortical sensation

= graphesthesia, stereognosis, tactile extinction, 2-point discrimination

« deficits in cortical sensation are often localized to the contralateral parietal lobe
+ Romberg test

= patient stands with eyes closed and feet together

= instability may indicate loss of proprioception causing sensory ataxia

Coordination and Gait Exam

+ upper limb coordination

= finger-to-nose test

= rapid alternating movements (repeatedly flip hand on palm of opposite hand)
+ lower limb coordination

«= heel-to-shin test

= rapid alternating movements (repeatedly tap foot on ground)

+ gait

= gait pattern: antalgic, hemiplegic, ataxic, apraxic, Parkinsonian, steppage, broad-based

= heel-to-toe (tandem) gait
= pull test for postural instability
