Prevention of Progression

+ control of HTN, DM (HbA Ic <7%), cardiovascular risk factors (e.g. smoking cessation, physical activity,
weight loss)

+ avoid nephrotoxins such as NSAIDs, COX2 inhibitors, IV contrast in patients with eGFR <60 mL/min/1.73 m*

+ address reversible causes of AKI

Management
* use a comprehensive approach to reduce risks of progression of CKD and its associated complications
. diet, physical activity (at least 150 mins/wk, or as tolerated), weight management and stop use of tobacco
roducts

maintaining a protein intake of 0.8 g/kg body weight/d is suggested for adults with CKD G3-G5. Not
recommended in children as protein is needed for growth. Literature is conflicted regarding use of
protein restriction in certain other populations
= Na* restriction (<2 g/d) if HTN, CHF, or oliguria are present
