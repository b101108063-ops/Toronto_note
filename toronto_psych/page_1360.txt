Management

Pro er documentation of the clinical encounter and rationale for management is essential
for higher risk patients (with a plan and intention to act, have access to lethal means, recent social stressors,
and symptoms suggestive of a psychiatric disorder)
= hospitalization should be strongly considered; assess decision-making capacity if patient demands to
leave hospital
= do not leave patient alone; remove potentially dangerous objects from room
= if patient refuses to be hospitalized, complete form for involuntary admission (Form 1) and must give
patient Form 30 to notify them of their admission (in Ontario)
for lower risk patients (not actively suicidal, with no active plan, or access to lethal means)
= discuss protective factors and supports in their life, remind them of what they live for, promote survival
skills that helped them through previous suicide attempts
«= start managing underlying problems if suicide risk is not imminent (e.g. psychiatric disorders,
psychosocial stressors)
