Workplace Legislation

+ universal across Canada for corporate responsibility in the workplace: reasonable precautions to ensure
a safe workplace, application of Workplace Hazardous Materials Information System (WHMIS), and
existence of joint health and safety committees in the workplace with representatives from workers and
management

+ jurisdiction in Canada is provincial (90% of Canadian workers), except for 16 federally-regulated industries
(e.g. airports, banks, highway transport) under the Canada Labour Code

+ Ontario’s Occupational Health and Safety Act

= sets out rights of workers and duties of employers, procedures for workplace hazards, and law
enforcement
