Economic Evaluatio

+ economic evaluations involve the assessment of the cost and effect trade-offs of any interventions,
programs, or policies that impact health outcomes. Health economic evaluations are designed to inform
social decisions in health care, alongside evidence from other sources

Cost Effectiveness Analysis (CEA)

+ compares two or more drugs, devices, tests, or procedures to find out which provides more outcomes for the
cost of treatment or which has the lowest cost for a given outcome

+ the incremental cost-effectiveness ratio (ICER) shows the extra cost of the more expensive intervention
compared to the cheaper intervention (numerator) divided by the difference in health outcome
(denominator)

+ the denominator is the outcome expressed in natural (health) units, such as years of life gained, lives saved,
or clinical events avoided or achieved
