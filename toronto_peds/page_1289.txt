Respiratory Distress

DYSPNEA

Definition
+ any sensation ofa difficulty in breathing

Approach

+ determine if patient is sick or not sick; ABCs

+ history: onset, previous episodes, precipitating events, associated symptoms, past medical/family history of
respiratory disease

+ physical exam: vitals, SpO2, evidence of cyanosis, respiratory, cardiovascular

+ investigations: CBC and differential, electrolytes, BUN, Cr, NP swab, VBG, CXR, ECG (based on clinical findings)
