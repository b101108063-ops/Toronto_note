Development

Global Developmental Delay

+ also known as Early Developmental Impairment

Definition

+ significant delay (at least 2 SDs below the mean with standardized tests) in at least 2 developmental
domains (gross motor, fine motor, speech/language, cognitive, social/personal, activities of daily living) in
achild <5 yr

* may predict a diagnosis of intellectual disability in the future

+ after age 5, intellectual and physical disabilities are described (no longer a development ‘delay’ as catch up
is not expected)

Epidemiology
+ up to 3% of pediatric population; 40-80% of cases have a defined etiology
