Hepatitis A Virus

+ RNAvirus

+ fecal-oral transmission; incubation period 4-6 wk

+ diagnosed by elevated transaminases, positive anti-HAV IgM

+ inchildren: characteristically asymptomatic

+ inadults: fatigue, nausea, arthralgia, fever, jaundice, hepatomegaly

+ can cause ALF and subsequent death (<1-5%)

+ can relapse (rarely), but never becomes chronic

+ treatment is supportive (no specific treatments available, disease is often self-limiting)
+ preventable by vaccination

+ post-exposure prophylaxis is available for exposed contacts
