Clinical Features

+ non-erosive gastritis is often asymptomatic (except with certain rare causes like CD), does not cause pain;
difficult to diagnose clinically or endoscopically - requires biopsy for diagnosis

+ erosive gastritis can cause bleeding (pain only if progresses to ulcers ~ rare); can be seen endoscopically
