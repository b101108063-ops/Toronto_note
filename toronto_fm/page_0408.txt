Erectile Dysfunction
+ see Urology, U33

Definition
+ >3 mo of consistent or recurrent inability to attain and/or maintain sufficient penile erection for sexual
performance

Epidemiology
+ ~20% of men age 40; ~50% of men age 70

Etiology

* organic: vascular (90%) (arterial insufficiency, atherosclerosis), endocrine (low testosterone, DM), anatomic
(structural abnormality, e.g. Peyronie's), neurogenic (postoperative, DM), medications (clonidine,
antihypertensives, SSRIs, tricyclic antidepressants, cimetidine, 5-a-reductase inhibitors)

+ psychogenic (10-20%) (depression, anxiety, stress)
