Definition
+ age-related disease characterized by decreased bone mass and increased susceptibility to fractures

Epidemiology
+ in Canada, affects 1 in 4 women and 1 in 8 men

Clinical Presentation
+ asymptomatic for many years
+ fragility or osteoporotic fractures

Approach to Clinical Assessment

+ identify risk factors on history and physical examination

+ history
= prior falls, fragility fractures, parental hip fractures, and gait or balance issues
= glucocorticoid use
