Sore Throat (Pharyngitis)

Definition
+ inflammation of the oropharynx

+ may be caused by a wide range of infectious organisms, most of which produce a self-limited infection with

no significant sequelae

Etiology

+ viral: adenovirus, rhinovirus, influenza virus, RSV, Epstein-Barr virus (EBV), coxsackie virus, herpes
simplex virus, cytomegalovirus (CMV), HIV

+ bacterial: B-hemolytic Streptococcus, Neisseria gonorrhoeae, Chlamydia pneumoniae, Mycoplasma
pneumoniae, Corynebacterium diphtheriae, Fusobacterium necrophorum

Epidemiology
