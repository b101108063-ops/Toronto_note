= consider if QRS >130 msec with LBBB morphology, LVEF <35%, and persistent symptoms despite OMT
= greatest benefit likely with marked LV enlargement, MR, QRS >150 msec
= CRI-P is indicated for patients eligible for resynchronization therapy but not ICD; if the patient is also
