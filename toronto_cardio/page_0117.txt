Torsades de Pointes
avariant of polymorphic VT that occurs in patients with baseline QT prolongation “twisting of the points”
looks like usual VT except QRS complexes “rotate around the baseline,” changing their axis and amplitude
usually starts following a post extrasystolic pause (“pause dependent”)
ventricular rate >100 bpm, usually 150-300 bpm
= usual onset after a post-PVC pause associated with “pause dependent” QT prolongation
etiology: occurs in association with prolonged QT intervals
= congenital long QT syndromes
= drugs: e.g, class IA (quinidine), class III (sotalol), phenothiazines (TCAs), erythromycin, quinolones,
antihistamines
= electrolyte disturbances: hypokalemia, hypomagnesemia, hypocalcemia
= nutritional deficiencies causing above electrolyte abnormalities
treatment: IV magnesium, temporary pacing, 8 blocker, correct the underlying cause of prolonged QT
« electrical cardioversion and ACLS if hemodynamic compromise
