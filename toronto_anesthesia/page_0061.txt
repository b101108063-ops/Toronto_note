Routine Monitors for All Cases

+ pulse oximeter, BP monitor, ECG, capnography (required for GA and deep procedural sedation, Ramsay
Sedation Scale 4-6), and an agent-specific anesthetic gas monitor when inhalational anesthetic agents are
used

+ the following must also be available: temperature probe, peripheral nerve stimulator, stethoscope,
appropriate lighting, spirometry, and manometer to measure ETT cuff pressure
