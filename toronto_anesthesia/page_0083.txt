Malignant Hyperthermia

+ hypermetabolic disorder of skeletal muscle
+ due to an uncontrolled increase in intracellular Ca’* (because of an anomaly of the ryanodine receptor that
regulates Ca** channel in the sarcoplasmic reticulum of skeletal muscle)
+ autosomal dominant inheritance
+ incidence of 1-5 in 100,000, may be associated with skeletal muscle abnormalities such as dystrophy or
myopathy
+ anesthetic drugs triggering MH include:
= all inhalational agents except nitrous oxide
+ depolarizing muscle relaxants: SCh
