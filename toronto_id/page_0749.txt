Prevention of HIV Infection

+ education, including harm reduction
= safer sexual practices: condoms for vaginal and anal sex, barriers for oral sex
«= harm reduction for IVDU: avoid sharing needles
+ prevention of vertical HIV infection: treatment with ART should be initiated prior to pregnancy or as early
as possible during pregnancy. The risk of vertical HIV transmission can be reduced to <1% if maternal ART
is started in a timely manner and the maternal viral load is undetectable prior to delivery
+ universal blood and body precautions for healthcare workers
= post-exposure prophylaxis (PEP) after occupational (e.g. needle-stick injury) and non-occupational
(e.g. consensual sex, sexual assault) exposure to HIV: 2- or 3-drug regimen initiated immediately (<72
h) after exposure and continuing for 4 wk
+ recent data has demonstrated efficacy of pre-exposure prophylaxis (oral PrEP or topical microbicides) in
preventing HIV
= PrEP strategies include, daily TDF/FTC or TAF/FTC and “PrEP on demand”, where medication is taken
as a double dose 12-24 h before anticipated exposure and then for 2 more doses 24 and 48 h later (i.e.
2-1-1 dosing) or injectable cabotegravir given every 2 months
+ ART associated with 96% reduction in risk of transmitting HIV to sexual partners
+ screening of blood and organ donation
