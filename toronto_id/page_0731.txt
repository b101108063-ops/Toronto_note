Bone and Joint Infections

Septic Arthritis

Definition
+ infection of one or more joints by pathogenic microbes
. Medical Emergency
Routes of Infection Septic arthritis is a medical emergency! If
+ hematogenous (most common) untreated, rapid joint destruction will occur

= from distant infection (e.g. abscesses, wound infection, and bacteremia)
+ direct inoculation via skin/trauma

= iatrogenic (e.g. surgery, arthroscopy, arthrocentesis, joint injection)

= trauma (e.g. open wounds around the joint, penetrating trauma)
+ contiguous spread (e.g. septic bursitis, osteomyelitis)

Etiology
+ gonococcal
= N. gonorrhoeae: previously accounted for 75% of septic arthritis in young sexually active adults

+ non-gonococcal
= S. aureus: affects all ages, rapidly destructive, accounts for most non-gonococcal cases of septic arthritis

in adults (especially in those with rheumatoid arthritis) Disseminated Gonococcal Infection Triad
= Streptococcus spp. (Group A and B) Migratory arthralgias

= Salmonella spp: associated with sickle cell disease
