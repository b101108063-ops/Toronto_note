COVID-19

Definitions and Etiology
+ anacute infectious respiratory disease caused by the SARS-CoV-2 virus
+ SARS-CoV-2 isan cnval ed, positive-sense, ssRNA virus

+ transmission: droplet and airborne transmission

+ incubation period 2-14 d, usually ~5 d

Pathophysiology

+ invasion of host cells via the viral spike protein which binds to angiotensin-converting enzyme 2 (ACE2)
expressed on the surface epithelium of the lungs

+ virus induced cytotoxic damage particularly to the alveolar epithelium

+ dysregulated immune response can lead to a cytokine storm causing organ failure or death

Clinical Features
+ can be asymptomatic (estimated to be 1 in 3 of those infected)
= children are more likely to be asymptomatic or to have mild disease

* most common: fever, fatigue, and dry cough

common: dyspnea, loss of smell and/or taste (may vary depending on the variant circulating), loss of
appetite, and myalgia
less common: nausea, vomiting, abdominal pain, sore throat, headache, and thromboembolic events
course: can range from mild disease (lasts 1-2 wk) to severe or critical disease (lasts 3+ wk); may be
complicated by post-COVID condition which is associated with a wide range of diverse symptoms
across multiple organ systems that may fluctuate in intensity, often exacerbated by mental and physical
overexertion

Diagnosis

+ nasopharyngeal swabs for RT-PCR (gold standard) or nasal/oral-nasal swabs for rapid antigen detection
oint of care tests which have lower sensitivity

+ lower respiratory specimens for RT-PCR

Treatment
+ for critically ill hospitalized adults >18 yr requiring ventilatory and/or circulatory support:
= dexamethasone PO or IV for 10 d (or until discharge)
tocilizumab if within 14 d of new COVID-19 diagnosis
= prophylactic low molecular-weight or unfractionated heparin
+ for hospitalized adults pier requiring low-flow supplemental 02:
= dexamethasone PO or IV for 10 d (or until discharge)
= remdesivir IV for 5d
= tocilizumab if serum CRP 275 mg/L + disease progression despite 24-48 h of recommended steroid
dose + within 14 d of new COVID-19 diagnosis
= prophylactic low molecular-weight or unfractionated heparin
+ for mild-moderate COVID-19 in adults >18 yr who do not require supplemental 0: in any setting:
= stratify by risk of hospitalization
= for patients with low risk of hospitalization:
* supportive care
= for patients with high risk of hospitalization:
¢ nirmatrelvir/ritonavir (Paxlovid) if presenting within 5 d of symptom onset
 remdesivir if presenting within 7 d of symptom onset
