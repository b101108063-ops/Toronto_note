LASER REFRACTIVE EYE SURGERY

* permanently alters corneal refractive properties by ablating tissue to change curvature of the cornea

+ used for correction of myopia, hyperopia, and astigmatism

+ common types include PRK, LASIK, and SMILE

+ potential risks/side effects: infection, under/overcorrection, increased glare/halo perception at night,
corneal haze (surface haze of PRK prevented by mitomycin application at surgery; interface haze is rarer
but may occur after other procedures), dry eyes (more common in LASIK than PRK), regression, and
corneal ectasia

+ LASIK-specific: flap complications such as flap striae, free cap (by destruction of hinge area), loss of flap,
traumatic flap dislocations, buttonhole flap, and epithelial ingrowth)
