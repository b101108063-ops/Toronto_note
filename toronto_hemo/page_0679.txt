Pathophysiology of Pernicious Anemia

* auto-Abs produced against IF or gastric parietal cells, latter leading to achlorhydria and lack of IF secretion

IF is required to stabilize Bi. as it passes through the bowel

decreased IF leads to decreased ileal absorption of Bi

may be associated with other autoimmune disorders (polyglandular endocrine insufficiency)
