Definition

+ a disease of low platelet count (see Thrombocytopenia, H7) secondary to aberrant immune responses

Terminology of ITP

+ primary: ITP (platelet count <100 x 10°/L) with no other cause of thrombocytopenia
+ secondary: ITP associated with another condition (e.g. HIV, HCV, SLE, or CLL)
+ drug-induced: platelet destruction caused by anti-platelet Abs secondary to drug exposure

Classification of Primary ITP
+ acute: 3 mo from diagnosis

+ persistent: 3-12 mo from diagnosis

+ chronic: >12 mo from diagnosis
+ refractory: post-splenectomy
