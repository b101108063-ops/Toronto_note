Hereditary Spherocytosis

Definition/Etiology

+ most common type of hereditary hemolytic anemia

+ abnormality in RBC membrane proteins (e.g. spectrin)

+ autosomal dominant (variable penetrance), can also be autosomal recessive or de novo
+ presents with hemolytic anemia, jaundice, splenomegaly

Investigations
+ CBC and differential blood film (shows spherocytes)
+ RBC dehydration and membrane loss result in elevated MCHC
+ osmotic fragility (increased)

+ molecular analysis for spectrin gene

+ U/S (splenomegaly and gallstones (pigment))

+ genetic counselling
